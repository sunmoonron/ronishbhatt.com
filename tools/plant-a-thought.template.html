How it works: Bip is a 7-million-parameter language model that lives entirely inside this page; there is no server. It was trained from scratch for this game: a word-level GPT (6 layers, 256 wide, an 8,192-word vocabulary) trained on TinyStories for 36,000 steps in 444 seconds on one A100, then quantised to int8 and inlined here (9.4 MB of weights). A card is a real memory, not a hint, and planting never changes the weights: the two words are run through the model on their own, and the keys and values its attention wrote for them are spliced into Bip's memory ahead of the story, at no position at all, so every later word can look at them while it decides what to say. Two copies are planted per card because one is easy to ignore. Taking a thought out deletes exactly those entries, and the rest of the memory closes over the gap. The engine is hand-written JavaScript with a key/value cache; the story-length dial sets how many words Bip writes before stopping.
