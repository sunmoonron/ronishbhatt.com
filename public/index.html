Aboutdraft

I build things to see how they work underneath. Lately that means mechanistic interpretability of small language models: reading a transformer's residual stream with its own weights, and watching how it keeps, revises and reuses what it has read.

Computer science (software engineering specialist) at the University of Toronto Scarborough; the courses are listed below. Before that: security, browsers, and a long trail of one-page experiments (everything else).

How this page worksdraft

This HTML is twenty-two lines plus one small script, and until you click something no library at all; everything else on the page is a Nostr event signed by this site's key and rendered in place. The page is pre-rendered from my relay at deploy, then your browser asks the relay again and rebuilds itself only if something changed, so edits appear live in every open tab. The chat is NIP-17 (NIP-59 gift wraps, NIP-44 encryption, NIP-13 proof of work), each block is a NIP-23 note you can open in any client through the ⌁ beside its heading, and the icon is the first sixteen hex digits of the signing key, one shade of green per digit.
