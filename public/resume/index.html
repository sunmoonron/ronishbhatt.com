Selected work, 2026

ronishbhatt.com: a website made of signed events2026
  • A 22-line HTML shell; layout, stylesheet and every content block are kind 30078 events signed by the site key, fetched from a personal strfry relay with public relays as backups, verified and assembled in the browser. Subresource integrity and import-map integrity end to end.
  • Content veiled with NIP-44 under a key that is the favicon itself: an 8 × 8 mosaic encoding 32 bytes. Server-side pre-render for first paint, a 3 KB vanilla boot script, hashed module names to survive CDN caching.
  • NIP-17 gift-wrapped chat and small experiments (a mural of visitors' wraps, a word garden) all gated by NIP-13 proof of work instead of accounts, enforced by tiered relay write policy.
NOSTR ORBIT: a Nostr history on the timechain2026
  • Maps any profile's events to Bitcoin block heights using the real difficulty schedule (about five blocks of error instead of tens of thousands from a naive average), with halving-era rings, NIP-03 attestation counts and OpenTimestamps awareness.
  • A voxel chain where every Bitcoin block is a cube (12 × 12 a day, 14 days an epoch, a coil turn a year), mempool-style epoch blocks whose contents are the notes as a treemap coloured by relay-reported engagement, a scrubbable replay with captions. Software 3D on a 2D canvas, no libraries, frames only on input.
