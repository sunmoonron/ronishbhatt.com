ronishbhatt.com, a website made of signed events2026
  • A 22-line HTML shell; layout, stylesheet and every content block are signed Nostr events fetched from a personal strfry relay with public relays as backups, verified and assembled in the browser, with subresource and import-map integrity end to end. Content veiled with NIP-44 under a key that is the favicon itself (an 8 × 8 mosaic encoding 32 bytes).
  • NIP-17 gift-wrapped chat and small experiments gated by NIP-13 proof of work instead of accounts, enforced by tiered relay write policy. Runs on a hardened NixOS host with Bitcoin Core and the relay behind Cloudflare Tunnel, operated through a zero-SSH dashboard.
NOSTR ORBIT, a Nostr history on the timechain2026
  • Maps any profile's events to Bitcoin block heights using the real difficulty schedule (about five blocks of error instead of tens of thousands from a naive average), with halving-era rings and NIP-03 attestation counts.
  • A voxel chain where every Bitcoin block is a cube (12 × 12 a day, 14 days an epoch, a coil turn a year), mempool-style epoch blocks whose contents are the notes as a treemap coloured by relay-reported engagement, and a scrubbable replay. Software 3D on a 2D canvas, no libraries.
