LATTICE

A feed that is not a list. Every event lands at its Bitcoin block: epoch, day, row, column, the way a number has digits. A block is a voxel, a day is a 12 × 12 layer, an epoch is a brick, the bricks coil up one turn a year. Replies are strings between voxels, reactions are sparks. Counts arrive before the events, so the shape shows on a slow link before a single note has loaded, and text mode is the same lattice with no canvas at all.
