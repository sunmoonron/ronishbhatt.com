Infinite 432 Generative Music

Single-file, offline Web Audio music generator. It does not play a recording; it synthesizes an evolving sound field after you press Start.



  

    What this file means by “infinite music”
    
It is a process, not a track. Each run creates a fresh seed, then schedules drones, filtered noise, quiet glints, tuning drift, stereo motion, room response, and long-form harmonic movement a few seconds ahead of real time. There is no imported audio and no fixed song-length loop.

    
The sound still uses periodic waves at the microscopic level because every musical pitch is vibration. The non-loop part is the composition layer: note choices, durations, panning, filters, envelopes, harmonic centers, and noise are generated continuously with non-matching time scales and memory against immediate repetition.

  


  

    Design questions answered before building
    
    
      
  • What counts as infinite? An open-ended scheduler whose next events are generated from rules, not a finite playlist.
    • 
      
  • How does the ear perceive it? Keep energy low, avoid sudden attacks at night, avoid excessive high-frequency sparkle, and leave space so masking does not become mush.
    • 
      
  • Why 432? It is an aesthetic reference pitch option. The file defaults to A4 = 432 Hz, but does not claim special medical effects.
    • 
      
  • What about Schumann resonance? 7.83 Hz is below normal pitch hearing, so it is used as extremely subtle amplitude/filter motion, not as a loud sub-bass tone.
    • 
      
  • How can it fit different settings? The modes change density, high-frequency cutoff, transient rate, fade time, and volume target.
    • 
      
  • How are repetitions handled? The generator remembers recent degrees, uses probabilistic choices, slow tonal-center drift, and irrational-ish interval timing instead of bar loops.
    • 
    

  


  
Safety note: this page cannot know your speaker/headphone dBA. Start low, especially on headphones or during sleep. It is art software, not a medical treatment or sleep device.
