📝 AI Glossary

🔤 Token

A chunk of text the AI processes. Can be a word, part of a word, or punctuation. Example: [[ might be one token, 100 another.

🧠 Feature

A specific concept the AI learned to recognize, like "opening brackets" or "we're inside a list". Represented by neuron activations.

📊 Representation

A list of numbers (vector) describing a feature. Example: [0.2, -1.3, 0.7, ...] might mean "inside nested brackets".

👁️ Attention

How the AI connects words. When predicting what comes next, it "attends" (looks back) at previous tokens to gather context.

📈 Logits / Probability

Scores assigned to every possible next word. Higher score = more likely prediction. Logits become probabilities via softmax.

📚 Layer

A stage in the AI's pipeline. Early layers see raw patterns; later layers understand abstract concepts.

🎯 Attention Head

A sub-component within a layer. Different heads specialize in different patterns (syntax, semantics, etc.).

🎓 Why This Matters for AI Safety

This demonstrates Context Dilution, a real phenomenon in Transformer models.

Even though the model can technically see the [[ at the start, the mathematical operation it uses (Softmax Attention) forces it to "spend" its attention budget across all tokens. When context grows too long, the budget allocated to critical instructions becomes too small to trigger the correct behavior.

This has real implications for:

  • 📝 Long document understanding
  • 🔐 Prompt injection attacks (hiding malicious instructions in noise)
  • ⚖️ AI reliability in safety-critical applications
  • 🧮 Mathematical reasoning over long sequences
