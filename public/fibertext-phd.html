Notebook

Claim: language can be represented as a fiber bundle over a byte or glyph stream. Grammar is one possible coordinate chart. The invariant object is the transport: what changes, bends, repeats, vanishes, and remains recoverable across scale.

What changed from the toy prototype

The unit is not a word or sentence. The unit is a boundary that emerges when the stream's coding rate, curvature, and memory residual change enough to justify a new patch.

Why compression appears

If text is a path, compression is not deletion. It is finding a shorter carrier whose transport preserves endpoints, high curvature, rare events, and control tokens such as negation and numbers.

Use cases

  • Cheap retrieval prefilters before embedding search.
  • Prompt compression that keeps reasoning pivots rather than frequent filler.
  • Text diffing by geometric deformation instead of edit distance alone.
  • Tokenizer-free segmentation experiments for byte-level models.
  • Interpretability probes that compare model trajectories with carrier geometry.

Compression contract

The demo is intentionally model-free. It cannot know truth. It can preserve geometric work: rare shocks, turns, endpoints, numbers, negation, and high-surprise chunks. A real thesis would train and evaluate whether that work correlates with semantic recoverability.

Existing boundary

Current work strongly supports geometric views of embeddings, token spaces, reasoning trajectories, and compression-driven segmentation. The speculative leap here is to make a deterministic alphabet-compatible carrier geometry that can be compared against learned geometry.
